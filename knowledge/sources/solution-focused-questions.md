# Solution-focused questions

## The idea
Solution-focused approaches spend less time analysing problems and more time noticing what already works and what a better future would look like.

## Useful questions
- Exceptions: "When was this problem a bit less bad? What was different then?"
- Scaling: "On a scale of 0 to 10, where 10 is how you'd like things to be, where are you now? What makes it that number and not lower? What would one point higher look like?"
- The preferred future: "Imagine you wake up tomorrow and things are a bit better. What's the first small thing you'd notice?"
- Coping: "Things have been hard. How have you managed to keep going?"
- Next steps: "What's the smallest step that would move you in the right direction?"

## Why it helps
These questions draw attention to strengths, resources and progress that are easy to overlook when struggling, and they make next steps concrete.
