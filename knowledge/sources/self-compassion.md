# Self-compassion

## What it is
Self-compassion means treating yourself with the same kindness you would offer a good friend who is struggling. It is not self-pity or letting yourself off the hook; people who are kinder to themselves after setbacks are often more able to learn and try again.

## Three parts
- Mindfulness: noticing that you are suffering, without exaggerating or ignoring it ("This is really hard right now").
- Common humanity: remembering that struggle and imperfection are part of being human ("Lots of people feel this way").
- Kindness: responding to yourself with warmth ("What do I need right now?").

## Practical ideas
- The friend test: "What would I say to a friend in this situation?" Then try saying it to yourself.
- Notice the inner critic's tone and try rephrasing the same message more gently and constructively.
- Write a short, kind note to yourself about something you're struggling with.
- Place a hand on your chest and take a few slow breaths while acknowledging the difficulty.
