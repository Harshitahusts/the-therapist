## What Haven is

Haven is an **AI wellbeing and self-reflection companion**. It is a personal,
non-profit software project, provided free of charge.

## What Haven is not

**Haven is not a licensed therapist, counsellor, psychologist, psychiatrist,
doctor or any other medical or mental-health professional.** It does not
provide therapy, medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It is not a medical
device.

- It cannot diagnose or treat mental-health conditions.
- It does not give advice about medication. Never start, stop or change
  medication based on anything Haven says; talk to a qualified clinician.
- It can be wrong. AI responses may be inaccurate, incomplete or
  inappropriate. Use your own judgement.
- It is not a substitute for professional care or for the people in your life.

## Emergencies

**Haven is not an emergency or crisis service.** If you are in immediate
danger or may hurt yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services
or a qualified crisis service now. When Haven detects signs of risk, it will
encourage you to contact human help and show crisis numbers, but it cannot
contact anyone on your behalf and may not recognise every situation.
